# Vehicle Service: Reports & Analytics Manual (Diataxis Framework)

This document provides complete documentation for the Vehicle Service Reports & Analytics module, structured according to the **Diataxis** documentation framework into **Tutorials**, **How-To Guides**, **Reference**, and **Explanation**.

## Part 1: Tutorials (Learning-Oriented)

### Tutorial 1: Getting Started with the Reports Dashboard
The **Reports Dashboard** (`reports-dashboard`) is the central executive cockpit for workshop managers, service advisors, and job controllers.

#### Step 1: Open the Dashboard
1. Log in to ERPNext desk.
2. Navigate to the **Vehicle Service** workspace.
3. Click on the **Reports Dashboard** shortcut in the top section or under **Analytics & Reports**.

#### Step 2: Set Filters & Date Range
1. Use the **From Date** and **To Date** pickers in the header filter bar to specify your reporting period (default is the past 30 days).
2. If managing multiple service centers, select a specific **Branch** from the dropdown, or leave it blank to view consolidated company totals.
3. Click **Refresh**.

#### Step 3: Analyze the 6 Core KPI Metric Cards
- **Completed Orders**: Total vehicles where repair work passed Quality Control and is stamped `Ready for Billing`.
- **Daily Throughput**: Daily rate of completed repairs and average vehicle turnaround time in workshop hours.
- **Current WIP Value**: Total open financial exposure in the shop (Parts at stock valuation rate + Labour at technician hourly cost).
- **Parts Sold**: Total net revenue generated by spare parts across workshop repairs and counter sales.
- **Labour Units Sold**: Total billable flat-rate hours credited to technicians.
- **DOC Gross Margin**: Combined gross profit percentage across parts and labor operations.

#### Step 4: Interact with Visual Analytics Charts
- **Throughput Trend**: Compares daily volume of vehicles Received, Started, and Completed.
- **Branch WIP Valuation**: Stacked bar comparison of Parts WIP vs. Labour WIP per workshop branch.
- **Technician Productivity**: Bar chart comparing Sold Units (billed hours) against Actual Hours clocked.

## Part 2: How-To Guides (Problem-Oriented)

### How to Conduct Work-in-Progress (WIP) Valuation and GL Warehouse Reconciliation
**Problem:** You need to audit active repair orders to verify unbilled costs and ensure operational parts in the workshop match the ERPNext General Ledger stock valuation.

1. Navigate to **Vehicle Service Workspace > Analytics & Reports > WIP Valuation**.
2. To audit individual repair orders:
   - Select **View Mode: Order Summary**.
   - Review each order's **Days in WIP** and **Aging Bucket** (`0-7 Days`, `8-14 Days`, `15-30 Days`, `30+ Days`).
   - Examine the split between **Parts WIP Cost**, **Labour WIP Cost**, and **Sublet WIP Cost**.
3. To audit consumed spare parts:
   - Switch **View Mode** to **Parts Details**.
   - Verify issued quantities, returned parts, and stock valuation rates (from inventory transactions).
4. To audit accrued labour:
   - Switch **View Mode** to **Labour Details**.
   - Check technician hourly cost rates (`Employee.custom_hourly_cost_rate`) and actual clocked hours.
5. To reconcile with the ERPNext Stock Ledger:
   - Switch **View Mode** to **Branch Reconciliation**.
   - Compare **Operational Parts WIP** against **Warehouse Stock Valuation** in `wip_warehouse`.
   - Inspect the **Variance** column; status will show `Reconciled` if variance is within $0.01.

---

### How to Analyze Technician Labour Units Sold and Efficiency
**Problem:** You need to determine technician billing productivity and identify mechanics who are beating flat-rate book times versus those running over.

1. Navigate to **Vehicle Service Workspace > Analytics & Reports > Technician Labour Units**.
2. Select the date range and optionally filter by **Branch** or a specific **Technician**.
3. Evaluate the columns:
   - **Sold Units (Hrs)**: Standard billed book time credited to the technician.
   - **Actual Hours**: Total clock hours logged on jobs by the technician.
   - **Efficiency Rate %**: Calculated as $\left(\frac{\text{Sold Units}}{\text{Actual Hours}}\right) \times 100$.
     - $\ge 100\%$: Technician is efficient (completing jobs faster than book time).
     - $< 100\%$: Technician took longer than estimated standard hours.
   - **Gross Profit**: Billed labour revenue minus technician direct internal labor cost.

---

### How to Track Parts Sales and Margins (Repair Order vs. Over-The-Counter)
**Problem:** You need to distinguish parts consumed on service repair orders from walk-in retail sales and monitor profitability.

1. Navigate to **Vehicle Service Workspace > Analytics & Reports > Parts Sales**.
2. Filter by **Sale Type**:
   - `Workshop Repair`: Parts issued to `Service Order` repair jobs.
   - `Over-the-Counter`: Spare parts sold directly via counter Sales Invoices.
3. Review **COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)**:
   - Verified against actual `tabStock Ledger Entry` incoming valuation rates.
4. Review **Gross Profit** and **Margin %** to ensure pricing targets are met.

---

### How to Review Daily Operating Control (DOC)
**Problem:** Senior management requires an executive daily overview of shop operations, revenue, cost burdens, and margins.

1. Navigate to **Vehicle Service Workspace > Analytics & Reports > Daily Operating Control**.
2. To inspect daily performance trends:
   - Select **View Mode: Daily Operating Trend**.
   - Inspect daily row-by-row throughput, parts revenue/GP, labour revenue/GP, and overall margin %.
3. To inspect an executive P&L breakdown:
   - Select **View Mode: Executive Summary**.
   - View formatted summary tables for Workshop Volume, Parts Department, Labour Department, Combined Workshop Total, and Balance Sheet WIP Snapshot.

## Part 3: Reference (Information-Oriented)

### 1. Calculation Formulas & Rules Dictionary

| Metric | Formula / Rule | Source / Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Completion Timestamp** | Stamped automatically when `status` transitions to `Ready for Billing`. | `Service Order.work_completed_on` |
| **Turnaround Time (Hrs)** | `work_completed_on - (work_started_on OR checked_in_on)` in decimal hours. | Excludes delays prior to physical intake. |
| **Daily Backlog** | Active orders with `intake_date <= T` and (`work_completed_on > T` or `NULL`). | Counts open repair volume at close of day $T$. |
| **Net Parts Consumed** | $\max(0, \text{qty\_issued} - \text{qty\_returned})$ | `tabService Order Part` |
| **Parts WIP Cost** | $\sum (\text{Net Qty Consumed} \times \text{Valuation Rate})$ | Valuation rate from `tabStock Ledger Entry` or `Item`. **Selling price strictly excluded.** |
| **Labour Hourly Cost Rate** | `Employee.custom_hourly_cost_rate` $\to$ fallback to `Vehicle Service Settings.default_labour_cost_rate` ($40.0 default). | Internal tech cost burden. **Customer billing rate strictly excluded.** |
| **Labour WIP Cost** | $\sum (\text{Actual Logged Hours} \times \text{Labour Hourly Cost Rate})$ | Accrued labor expense on open orders. |
| **Single-Tech Units Sold** | 100% of `Service Job.estimated_hours` credited to assigned technician. | Preserves job-level isolation across order. |
| **Shared-Tech Units Sold** | $\text{Job Units} \times \left(\frac{\text{Tech Logged Hours}}{\text{Total Job Logged Hours}}\right)$ | Only split when multiple techs log on the *same* job. |
| **Labour Efficiency %** | $\left(\frac{\text{Sold Units}}{\text{Actual Hours}}\right) \times 100$ | Standard flat-rate productivity measure. |
| **Parts COGS** | $\text{Sold Qty} \times \text{Stock Valuation Rate}$ | Stock Ledger incoming rate on delivery/invoice. |
| **Gross Profit (GP)** | $\text{Net Sales Revenue} - \text{Cost (COGS or Labour Cost)}$ | Computed per line and departmentally. |
| **Gross Margin %** | $\left(\frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Sales Revenue}}\right) \times 100$ | Profit margin percentage. |

## Part 4: Explanation (Understanding-Oriented)

### 1. Centralized Calculation Engine Architecture
In complex ERP systems, calculating KPIs independently across various reports often leads to subtle discrepancies (e.g., one report using `creation` date while another uses `posting_date` or `modified`).

To guarantee 100% mathematical consistency:
- All business logic, queries, and financial formulas are centralized inside `vehicle_service.analytics` (`apps/vehicle_service/vehicle_service/analytics.py`).
- Desk pages, script reports, and background jobs invoke this single shared engine.
- If a calculation rule changes (e.g., updating the default labor cost rate or aging bucket thresholds), updating `analytics.py` updates every report, chart, and KPI card synchronously.

### 2. Separation of Cost Valuation vs. Customer Billing Rates
A fundamental rule in dealership and workshop management is the absolute separation between:
1. **Internal Cost (Valuation)**:
   - Parts: What the business paid for the inventory (stock valuation rate from inventory ledger).
   - Labour: What the business pays the technician per hour (hourly payroll burden).
2. **Customer Price (Billing)**:
   - Parts: Retail customer selling rate.
   - Labour: Customer flat-rate shop labor rate (e.g. $100–$150/hr).

**WIP (Work-In-Progress) is a balance sheet asset representing accumulated costs.** Valuing WIP at customer selling price creates fictitious balance sheet inflation and invalidates general ledger reconciliation. Our engine strictly values WIP using stock valuation rates and internal labor cost rates.

### 3. Multi-Branch Security & Governance Model
Workshops operating across multiple locations require both localized operational focus and centralized executive governance:
- **Server-Side Enforcement**: In `analytics.py`, `enforce_branch_permission()` inspects the logged-in session user.
- If a user lacks managerial permissions (`Service Manager`, `System Manager`, `Accounts Manager`), their user default `branch` is automatically applied as a server-side filter, preventing unauthorized cross-branch data access.
- Managerial roles have the authority to query company-wide consolidated metrics or filter down to any individual branch.
